How it works
STEP 1

Scale & Elbow

Features are standardized and K-Means is run across a range of k values, plotting inertia to visualize the elbow.

STEP 2

Validate with Silhouette

Silhouette scores confirm cluster cohesion & separation, auto-selecting the best k unless you override it.

STEP 3

Persona Generation

Centroids are mapped to income/spend tiers and translated into named personas with marketing strategies.
